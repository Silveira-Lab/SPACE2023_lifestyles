# SPACE2023_lifestyles
Ecology of viruses in meromictic lakes with a focus on host association and lifestyles.
## Sample used in analysis
### Metagenomes & MAGs,
All metagenomes were processed according the JGI's standard workflows including QC of reads, assembly, and binning [(Clum et al, 2021)](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/msystems.00804-20).
### Transcriptomes
All transcriptomes were processed according to [Varona et al, 2025](https://microbiomejournal.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s40168-025-02085-y)
### Hi-C Sequencing
Hi-C libraries were prepared with the Phase Genomics ProxiMeta Hi-C v4.0 Kit, briefly, DNA within cells was crosslinked with formaldehyde, digested with Sau3AI and MlucI, and ligated to form chimeric DNA molecules, which were then pulled down with streptavidin beads [(Varona et al, 2024)](https://academic.oup.com/ismej/article/18/1/wrae240/7918320); [Press et al, 2017](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/198713v1); [Uritsky et al, 2021](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2021.06.14.448389v1.abstract))
